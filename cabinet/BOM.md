## Tools you'll need besides the CNC

Drill/driver, 6 mm and 3 mm drill bits, a 14 mm bit for the M10 inserts, a countersink, clamps (at least 4 x 600 mm), a square, a roundover router bit (R9) for the lockbar, a soldering iron, crimpers and a multimeter.

## CNC tooling

- 6 mm downcut or compression spiral bit for the plywood (clean top face). The pockets and dados are sized for a 6 mm bit.
- 6 mm single-flute O-flute for the acrylic (slow plunge, high feed, keep the protective film on).
- 60 or 90 degree V-bit for the engraving.
